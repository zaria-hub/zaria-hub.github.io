Comparative Study of DQN, Double DQN, and Dueling DQN with Parameter Sharing for Adaptive Traffic Signal Control in Urban Multi-Intersection Environments

Central Research Question:

Among Classical DQN, Double DQN, and Dueling DQN ( all equipped with Prioritized Experience Replay and applied within a unified multi-intersection framework ) ,which architecture delivers the best adaptive traffic signal control performance under high-demand urban conditions, and what are the algorithmic reasons for the observed differences?

Proposed Solution (Overview)

A rigorous, fair, and comprehensive comparative evaluation of three value-based DRL architectures applied to adaptive traffic signal control , designed so that any observed difference in performance is attributable exclusively to architectural distinctions.
